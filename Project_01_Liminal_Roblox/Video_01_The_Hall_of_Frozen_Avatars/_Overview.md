# Video 01: The Hall of Frozen Avatars

**Genre:** Roblox / Liminal Weirdcore Suspense  
**Target Platform:** YouTube Shorts / Instagram Reels / TikTok  
**Format:** 9:16 Vertical Portrait  
**Total Target Runtime:** ~55–60 seconds (8 shots, trimmed to voice lines)  
**Narration Mode:** Pure Off-Screen Voice-Over (Voice: `Liminal Narrator`, characters never move lips)  
**Aesthetic Anchor:** Low-poly Roblox 3D, retro plastic textures, eerie buzzing fluorescent backrooms  

---

## Storyline & Retention Architecture

### 1. The 2-Second Hook (Shot 01)
> *"If you ever load into an empty server with zero ambient noise... do not touch your keyboard."*
Instantly hooks the viewer with an urgent rule of survival in an eerie liminal environment.

### 2. The Unanswered Question & Escalation (Shots 02–06)
The Wanderer explores the endless corridor and discovers other blocky avatars permanently frozen mid-stride or facing corners like digital mannequins. The narrator reveals the terrifying logic of the realm: each frozen avatar was once an active player who made the fatal mistake of pausing to type or check the menu. As the Wanderer walks, their own movement animation starts glitching and slowing down.

### 3. The Climax & Infinite Loop Ending (Shots 07–08)
The Wanderer reaches the end of the corridor and turns around in panic, only to see the exact identical perspective where they began—now standing beside another newly frozen avatar. The narration concludes with:
> *"Because if you stop walking... the server assumes you were always part of the wall."*
This line cuts right into the silent corridor of Shot 01, looping endlessly on platform feeds for exponential algorithm watch-time!
